## Design Context

### Users
A general audience exploring French legislative geography and comparing political scenarios. They need to understand what maps show and what estimates can and cannot establish.

### Brand Personality
Editorial, civic, thoughtful. The experience should feel approachable and credible, with room for curiosity.

### Aesthetic Direction
Match the existing French political atlas: a printed editorial sensibility, warm paper tones, restrained political colors, display typography, and a map-first composition. Preserve the established visual language while making comparisons clear and readable across screen sizes.

### Design Principles
- Put the map and the relevant comparison first; reveal technical detail progressively.
- Clearly distinguish observed results, first-round leads, and modeled runoff estimates.
- Surface uncertainty and coverage without implying guarantees.
- Use color alongside text and accessible controls; support keyboard and touch.
- Maintain visual continuity with the existing atlas and its editorial tone.
