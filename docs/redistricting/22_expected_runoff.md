# Nationwide partisan screening and simulated-seat objective

This run replaces the deterministic winner-count objective of [21](21_shared_runoff_search.md) for the displayed partisan maps. It assesses all 101 departmental territories in the local graph, retaining the official district count in every changed department. The 11 constituencies of French citizens abroad are not part of this departmental search and, together with other uncovered seats, remain among the 18 seats without simulated estimates.

The earlier 40 departments were re-used; the other 61 were screened for polling-station-to-unit coverage and a validated starting partition. In total **75 departments** passed the screening and were searched. Fifteen failed the 99% minimum coverage condition for at least one split area: 06, 13, 31, 33, 35, 44, 59, 67, 69, 75, 92, 93, 94, 95 and 974. Eleven more had no valid start produced by the bounded search: 17, 21, 22, 29, 54, 56, 65, 84, 85, 971 and 976. A second attempt with a 45-second start-generation limit did not produce a valid start for those 11. These statuses do not prove a valid map cannot exist. Unchanged departments retain official boundaries.

The new objective for a bloc is the sum, across departmental districts, of its modeled probabilities of winning after drawing an empirical residual for the hypothesized second-round duel. These probabilities are computed exactly from the same residual pools used by the full model's simulations; a test independently compares the fast scorer with the full model. Candidate maps are still ranked using first-round share only as a tie-breaker. Annealing follows the expected-seat objective. The existing target-specific searches share each valid candidate across blocs, and ReCom prioritizes potentially competitive districts while retaining unguided moves. This run uses one round, two seeds and 40 proposals per method and seed, starting from prior shared-run candidates where available. It is a bounded exploratory search, not a global optimum.

For each displayed scenario, the full model draws 1,000 simulated outcomes. The 10th–90th percentile ranges are shown instead of the deterministic count of district-level 50% winners. The map and hemicycle use **the same single simulated outcome**, selected to minimize the sum of absolute distances from bloc-wise simulation medians. It is an illustration, not a forecast or a guarantee that every individual bloc count equals its median. The simulation intervals are conditional on this model and its residual draws; they do not include uncertainty from searching for maps, constituency-level aggregation, future votes or correlated shocks. Some duel types have very little historical support, and redrawn-election behavior cannot be validated from the 2024 result alone.

`22_expected_search_runs.tar.gz` contains the original 101-department expected-seat report and both screening reports; each `22_<bloc>_expected.json` records selected validated departmental assignments and a source-report hash. `22_expected_compactness.json` records the independently reconstructed territorial metric. Candidate vote data remain in the local quarantine. The population constraints and topology are model checks, not a legal certification of the composite national map.
