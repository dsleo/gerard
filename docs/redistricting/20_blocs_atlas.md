# Electoral families used in the atlas

The 2024 electoral maps, the neutral estimate and the partisan estimates use a common partition of the candidate votes. The grouping is an analytical choice, not an official statement about a candidate's ideology.

| Display label | Classification in the 2024 election data | Examples and qualifications |
|---|---|---|
| Extrême gauche | EXG nuance, plus any candidate financially attached to LFI | Includes Lutte ouvrière (EXG) and LFI-attached candidates, including those running under the UG union label. Financial attachment is not proof of party membership. |
| Gauche | COM, FI, SOC, RDG, VEC, UG, DVG, ECO, except candidates financially attached to LFI | Includes socialists, communists, ecologists and other left-union candidates, as identified by their 2024 electoral nuance. |
| Centre | REN, MDM, HOR, ENS, DVC, UDI | Includes Renaissance, MoDem and Horizons, and also centre and UDI nuances. UDI is a defensible alternative classification. |
| Droite républicaine | LR, DVD, DSV | Includes Les Républicains, other right-wing and sovereignist-right nuances. The placement of DSV is a judgment call. |
| Extrême droite | RN, REC, UXD, EXD | Includes RN, Reconquête and the relevant union and far-right nuances. |
| Autres | REG, DIV and any unmatched nuance | A residual category, not a sixth political bloc. |

Candidate-level financial attachment was joined to each candidate and validated against official first-round candidate totals. In the current parliamentary-assembly view, the data represent deputies' current parliamentary groups instead: the LFI group is displayed as extrême gauche; LIOT and non-inscrits remain in the residual category. A parliamentary group and a 2024 candidate's financial attachment are different facts, so those classifications need not agree for every individual.

For the scenarios the first round aggregates bloc votes, then the second-round model selects two leading blocs for a hypothetical duel. The results are estimates, not observed elections on the redrawn maps. For partisan optimization details and limitations see [the runoff experiment](19_runoff_optimization.md).
