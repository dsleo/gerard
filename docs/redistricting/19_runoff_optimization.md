# Partisan scenarios optimized for the modeled second round

This experiment targets the number of seats assigned to each bloc by the atlas's 2024 nearest-duel second-round model, rather than first-round leads. It screens 40 departments with saved valid starts; 34 met the minimum 99% polling-station allocation coverage. Six were excluded: 06, 13, 31, 44, 67 and 75. Within eligible departments, previously saved valid maps seed two search methods (annealed ReCom and greedy boundary flips), with two random seeds and 80 proposals per method in the first batch and 120 in the expanded batch. Each best-found replacement is independently checked for contiguity, population tolerance and seat count. Positive modeled gains are additive because the model is fitted once and unchanged departments retain their official map. The search is neither exhaustive nor a national optimum.

| Target | Current map, modeled seats | Best found, modeled seats | Changed departments |

| Far left (LFI attachment or EXG nuance) | 74 | 85 | 7 |
| Other left | 109 | 131 | 17 |

| Right | 79 | 89 | 9 |
| National right | 115 | 159 | 25 |

The 559 modeled constituencies exclude 18 seats without estimates. Both columns apply the same model to 2024 votes. They are not actual election outcomes and do not establish that these maps would win seats in a new election. Values are optimized against the model and therefore particularly susceptible to model error and selection bias. Changed districts use polling-station-to-IRIS allocation; even eligible departments can have unallocated votes. Model simulation intervals do not include uncertainty from the map search or allocation. The central seat count can fall outside the simulation interval: selecting maps for central wins can produce many marginal model predictions that reverse when residual errors are sampled. This is a warning against interpreting the optimized central gain as robust.

The six-bloc experiment separates candidates financially attached to LFI from the other left candidates before pooling 2024 votes, including candidates with the shared `UG` nuance. A financial attachment is not party membership. The far-left bloc also includes candidates with the EXG nuance; COM and other non-LFI-attached left candidates remain in the remaining left bloc. The centre includes REN, MDM, HOR, ENS, DVC and UDI; the right includes LR, DVD and DSV; the national right includes RN, REC, UXD and EXD; REG and DIV are residual. These classifications are judgment calls. The analog model has few historical duels for some pairs involving LFI or the remaining left, and falls back to first-round shares when fewer than five comparable duels exist. Current, neutral and partisan electoral views use the same six-way grouping, including the residual category. The current parliamentary hemicycle uses present parliamentary groups rather than 2024 candidate affiliations.

The saved search reports are in `19_runoff_search_runs.tar.gz`; `19_<bloc>_runoff.json` contains each chosen hybrid map and department scores. The fast search scorer was checked against the full runoff model on all modeled official constituencies. The browser snapshots are recalculated independently by the full model. Compactness uses the same Lambert-93 reconstruction and baseline check documented in [territorial metrics](17_partisan_territorial_metrics.md).
